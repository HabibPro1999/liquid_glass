## 1.0.0

* Initial release.
* Full glass shader pipeline with SDF shapes (squircle, roundedRect, circle, capsule, ellipse).
* Two-tier rendering: Impeller (Tier 1) and blur+specular fallback (Tier 2).
* LiquidGlassGroup for shared backdrop and shape merging.
* LiquidGlassButton with press-scale animation and style presets.
* GlassTheme for app-wide glass defaults.
* Accessibility auto-detection (high contrast, reduced motion, dark mode).
* ShapeBorder adapter for Flutter shape interop.
